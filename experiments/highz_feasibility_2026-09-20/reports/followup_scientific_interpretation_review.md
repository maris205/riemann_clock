# Scientific interpretation review of the spectroscopy follow-up

Reviewed 2026-09-21. Scope: `paper/espresso_followup_section.tex`, the new abstract sentence in `paper/main.tex`, and completed ESPRESSO empirical-GLS/core-mask outputs available at review time. No manuscript, fit, or result files were changed. Exact numerical verification belongs to the separate follow-up audit. Final UVES numbers were still pending and are not evaluated here.

**Disposition: the scientific framing is sound, with two wording clarifications recommended before final delivery and one final-result selection requirement.** No passage currently establishes a varying physical constant or an inverse-logarithmic time law.

## Items to resolve

1. **Make the local optimizer limitation explicit in this follow-up section.** The completed ESPRESSO fits predominantly stop on relative objective change (`ftol`), retain bound-active nuisance parameters, and can differ between starting points. The preceding section already disclaims global minima, but the new discussion of actual nonlinear re-fitting should carry this limitation directly. Suggested sentence: “These are bounded local solutions selected from the tested initializations; objective-change stopping, active nuisance bounds and competing gas decompositions do not certify a global minimum or complete first-order stationarity.” Do not infer convergence solely from `optimizer_success=true`, nor quote the last decimals as a physical precision.

2. **Describe the continuum exclusions by their actual implementation.** Replace “exclude all three published absorption-fitting regions” with “exclude all wavelength intervals listed in the authors’ three published fit files.” The code excludes the union of 35 specified wavelength intervals, not three continuous global velocity bands around every line. This is a precision improvement; the actual retained control pixels were independently verified.

3. **Freeze the best completed fits before emitting table macros and figures.** At review time, a successful continuation of the core-0.1 alternative already had a lower objective than the alternative named in its comparison JSON. This is an output-selection dependency while work is ongoing, not a scientific conclusion or a reason to publish provisional numbers. Regenerate the comparison after final selection and let the numerical reviewer verify the table, macros, and plots against those selected files.

An optional abstract refinement is to replace “weaken the preference” with “reduce the conditional fit improvements.” Both reflect the current qualitative direction, but the latter more clearly describes what was measured without suggesting a calibrated change in detection probability.

## Interpretation that passes

- **Raw versus tapered correlation:** the measured lag-1 value is 0.372639, while the nonlinear covariance kernel uses 0.338763 after multiplication by 10/11. The displayed equation states that taper correctly; the prose does not confuse the two. Defining `rho_hat_0 = 1` explicitly would make the diagonal normalization immediately clear. The measured lag-2 value 0.0440 supports the stated warning against treating AR(1) as a measured complete covariance law.
- **Covariance transfer and variance:** the nonlinear code uses supplied per-pixel errors multiplied by the fixed tapered correlation shape. The 0.7048 continuum variance factor is separate. Scaling an otherwise fixed covariance by that factor divides both quadratic objectives and their difference by it without changing exact minimizing parameters. The text correctly declines to treat that transfer as a calibrated deep-absorption noise correction. The interval-bootstrap bounds remain conditional on the exploratory selected controls and do not account for every selection or instrumental systematic.
- **Continuum correlation versus a physical signal:** controls demonstrate correlated fluctuations outside the gas-model residuals. They do not establish that those fluctuations are exclusively resampling noise, and they do not establish a physical frequency change. The present wording respects this boundary.
- **Core masks:** the masks are frozen from an earlier fitted model, applied identically to both hypotheses, and do not follow individual noisy pixels or move during optimization. They remain data-informed exploratory masks. The explicit statement that removing cores also removes velocity information is essential and correct: a smaller improvement cannot identify saturation as the explanation. The text also correctly separates diagonal-error masking tests from full-pixel GLS instead of implying both modifications were jointly tested.
- **Nonlinear versus local results:** the follow-up paragraph distinguishes the new constrained nonlinear fits from the earlier tangent-space projected score. No Wilks significance or discovery probability is claimed.
- **Instrument and epoch independence:** UVES uses a different instrument but the same absorber, laboratory inputs and a closely related gas description. It cannot provide a second cosmological epoch or make shared astrophysical/model uncertainties independent. The text says the comparison does not independently establish the shifts and explicitly withholds a cosmic-time inference. The final UVES result must preserve that wording even if its sign eventually agrees with ESPRESSO.
- **Recent HARPS method update:** the [primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06809) confirms submission on 7 July 2026. Its [Section 5](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.06809v1) describes 36 observations from December 2018 totaling 52 h 31 min. Describing this as a new processing method applied to historical exposures is correct. “Did not locate ... in the checked release channels” is appropriately narrower than claiming the products do not exist. No HARPS fit or independent confirmation should be implied without those products.

This review does not certify final optimizer convergence or current table numbers. Subject to the items above and the separate numerical audit, the section supports the restrained conclusion that the relative-shift pattern is sensitive to ordinary noise/model choices and remains unresolved; it does not yet exceed all conventional explanations.
